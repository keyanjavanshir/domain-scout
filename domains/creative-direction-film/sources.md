# Sources

checked: 2026-11-20

All text in this pack is paraphrased. Each source below informed the parts listed.

## Practitioner guides

- https://www.shutterstock.com/blog/video-production-process — practitioner; the three
  production phases and pre-production artefacts (brief, script, storyboard, shot list).
- https://www.soundstripe.com/blogs/three-stages-of-video-production — practitioner; call
  sheets and shooting to the shot list.
- https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/video/production — vendor guide; post-production roles
  (editor, colorist, sound) and the shot list driving the schedule.
- https://www.premiumbeat.com/blog/creative-agencies-need-creative-director-of-video —
  practitioner; the creative director's place in agency video work and overlap with directing.
- https://blog.celtx.com/what-does-a-creative-director-do-in-film/ — practitioner; how the CD
  role differs from art director and production designer.
- https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/producer-vs-director — practitioner; producer and
  director responsibilities.

## Standards, regulators and legal guidance

- https://www.wipo.int/web-publications/rights-clearance-a-guide-for-independent-filmmakers/en/annex-ii-checklist.html
  — international body; rights clearance and E&O paperwork.
- https://human.libretexts.org/Courses/City_College_of_San_Francisco/Video_Production_Handbook_I/02%3A_Releases_and_Contracts
  — course text; talent and location releases.
- https://www.videomaker.com/how-to/legal/permission/what-you-need-to-know-about-video-production-and-the-law/
  — practitioner; sync versus master music licences, E&O and liability insurance.
- https://tech.ebu.ch/docs/r/r128v4_0.pdf — standard; EBU R128 loudness target.
- https://www.atsc.org/atsc-documents/a85-techniques-for-establishing-and-maintaining-audio-loudness/
  — standard; ATSC A/85 loudness for US broadcast (2026-07 revision).
- https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/time-based-media.html — standard; WCAG 2.2
  captions (1.2.2) and audio description (1.2.5).
- https://www.iab.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/IAB_AI_Trasparency_Disclosure_Framework_August_2026.pdf
  — industry body; when AI use in ads needs disclosure, and C2PA as the metadata route.
- https://c2pa.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/33/2026/07/Use-of-Content-Credentials-to-Identify-Synthetic-and-Non-Synthetic-Content.pdf
  — standard body; Content Credentials for synthetic media.
